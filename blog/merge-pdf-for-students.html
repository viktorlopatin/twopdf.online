How to Merge PDF Files for a University Submission

A practical workflow for putting reports, covers, scans, and appendices into one PDF.

Updated September 15, 2026·5 min read

When do students need to merge PDFs?

Common situations include submitting a lab report with a cover page, attaching a signed document, combining scanned pages, or putting an appendix behind a main report. The files may already be PDFs; they just need to become one document.

Put the files in the right order

A typical university submission might be:

  1. Cover page or title page
  2. Main report or assignment
  3. Tables, figures, or supporting pages
  4. Appendix or scanned evidence

Merge them with TwoPDF

  1. Open TwoPDF in Telegram.
  2. Send the PDF files.
  3. Follow the bot's merge workflow.
  4. Download the finished PDF and submit it.

Final checklist

  • All required pages are included.
  • The cover page is in the right position.
  • Pages are readable and correctly oriented.
  • The final filename is clear.
  • The PDF opens normally before submission.
