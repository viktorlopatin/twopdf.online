How to Combine Scanned Documents Into One PDF

A simple way to combine scanned pages, document images, and separate PDF scans into one organized PDF.

Scanning a multi-page document can sometimes result in many separate files instead of one complete document. You might have a separate PDF or image for every page, especially when documents are scanned using a phone or a scanner app.

When you need to share or store the complete document, having dozens of separate files can be inconvenient. Combining the scanned pages into one PDF creates a single document that is easier to read, save, and send.

Why combine scanned documents into one PDF?

A single PDF can make scanned documents easier to manage. Instead of keeping every scanned page as a separate file, you can put the pages together in their original order.

  • Keep all pages of a scanned document in one file.
  • Organize scanned paperwork before sharing it.
  • Combine several scanned pages into a single PDF.
  • Make large collections of scanned documents easier to manage.
  • Send a complete scanned document as one attachment.

What scanned documents can you combine?

The same workflow can be used for many types of scanned paperwork. You can combine separate PDF scans as well as scanned document images when creating a single PDF.

  • Scanned forms
  • Contracts and agreements
  • Receipts and invoices
  • Certificates
  • Identification documents
  • School and university documents
  • Printed notes and handwritten documents
  • Multi-page paperwork

Put the scanned pages in the correct order

Before combining the files, check that the scanned pages are arranged in the correct order. This is especially important when a document contains many pages.

For example, a ten-page scanned document should appear as:

  • Page 1
  • Page 2
  • Page 3
  • Page 4
  • Page 5
  • Page 6
  • Page 7
  • Page 8
  • Page 9
  • Page 10

Checking the order before creating the final PDF can help prevent pages from appearing in the wrong position.

Combine scanned PDFs using Telegram

If your scanned pages are already saved as PDF files, you can use TwoPDF through Telegram to combine them into one document. Send the PDF files to the bot and receive a single combined PDF.

This can be useful when a scanner or document app has created a separate PDF for each page or section of a document.

Turn scanned images into one PDF

Scanned documents are not always saved as PDF files. A phone or scanner may save each page as a JPG or PNG image instead.

In that situation, the images can be converted into a PDF and combined into a single document. This is useful when you have several photos or scanned images that belong to the same multi-page document.

Check the final scanned document

After combining the pages, open the final PDF and check that all pages are present and appear in the correct order. Make sure that the scanned pages are readable and that no important page was accidentally left out.

If the document is going to be submitted to a university, company, government office, or another organization, also check whether there are any specific requirements for the final file.

Share the complete scanned document

Once the pages are combined, the final PDF can be stored or shared as a single file. This is often more convenient than sending a large number of separate scanned pages.
