How to Combine Documents for a Job Application

A simple way to turn several application files into one organized PDF before you send them to an employer.

A job application can include a resume, cover letter, certificates, references, and other supporting documents. When these files are stored separately, it is easy to lose track of attachments or send them in the wrong order.

Put your application files in the right order

Before combining your documents, decide how you want the final PDF to look. A common workflow is to place the resume first, followed by the cover letter and then any certificates or supporting documents. Give the files clear names and make sure each page is readable before merging them.

TwoPDF lets you combine several PDFs into one document through Telegram. This is useful when your application files are already on your phone and you want to prepare one attachment without opening a full desktop PDF editor.

Typical use cases

  • Combining a resume, cover letter, and references into one PDF.
  • Adding diplomas, certificates, or other proof of qualifications to an application.
  • Joining scanned documents into a single attachment before sending it to a recruiter.

Check the final PDF before sending

After merging the files, quickly review the document from the first page to the last. Check that the pages are in the intended order, that no document is missing, and that the final file opens correctly on your phone or computer.
