How to Combine Lecture Notes Into One PDF

A simple way to combine lecture notes, presentations, handouts, and other study materials into one PDF file.

University courses can quickly leave you with a large collection of separate PDF files. A single course may include lecture notes, presentation slides, reading materials, worksheets, and additional documents. Keeping all of these files together can make studying and sharing course materials more difficult.

Combining your lecture notes into one PDF can make the collection easier to organize, store, and read. Instead of opening many separate files, you can keep the materials for a course or subject in one document.

Why combine lecture notes into one PDF?

Students often receive course materials as separate files throughout a semester. Combining them can be useful when you want to create a single study document before an exam or keep all materials for a particular subject together.

  • Keep lecture notes and presentation slides in one file.
  • Organize materials for a specific course or subject.
  • Make exam preparation easier by keeping study materials together.
  • Share several lecture documents as a single PDF.
  • Reduce the number of separate files in your phone or computer.

What can you combine?

You can combine different PDF documents that belong to the same course or topic. For example, you might have a PDF for each lecture and want to turn them into one complete set of notes.

  • Lecture notes
  • Presentation slides
  • Course handouts
  • Reading materials
  • Practice exercises
  • Study guides
  • Scanned notes and documents

Organize your files before combining them

Before combining the files, it is useful to put them in the order you want them to appear in the final document. For example, you might arrange lecture notes by week or follow the order of your university course.

A simple structure could be:

  • Lecture 1
  • Lecture 2
  • Lecture 3
  • Lecture 4
  • Additional reading
  • Practice materials

This way, the final PDF becomes a single organized collection that you can use throughout the semester.

Useful for exam preparation

Combining lecture notes can be especially useful before exams. Instead of searching through folders for individual presentations and notes, you can keep the relevant materials in one PDF and work through them in order.

You can also create a separate PDF for each subject, semester, or exam. This makes it easier to keep large collections of study materials organized.
