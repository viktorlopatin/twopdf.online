How to Merge Photos Into a PDF Document

A simple way to combine several photos into one PDF when you need a single document to share, submit, or store.

Photos are often the easiest way to capture paperwork, notes, receipts, forms, or other documents. The problem starts when you have many separate images and need to send them as one file. A PDF can keep those pages together and make the whole document easier to handle.

Why put photos into one PDF?

Keeping related images in separate files can make an attachment harder to organize. Combining them into one PDF gives you a single document with the pages kept together. This can be useful when you are preparing paperwork, sharing scanned notes, or sending several photographed pages at once.

Keep your photos in the right order

Before creating the PDF, decide how you want the images to appear. For example, if you photographed several pages of the same document, make sure the first page comes first and the remaining pages follow in order.

  • Review the photos before combining them.
  • Remove duplicate or unnecessary images.
  • Put the pages in the order you want to read them.
  • Make sure the important parts of each photo are visible.

Check the final PDF before sending it

After the photos have been combined, open the finished PDF and check the pages from beginning to end. Make sure the images are in the expected order and that no page was left out or added twice.

It is also worth checking that text, signatures, numbers, and other important details are clearly visible in the final document. A quick review can prevent problems when the PDF is submitted or shared.
