How to Combine Course Materials Into One PDF

A simple way to combine lecture notes, presentations, handouts, assignments, and other course materials into one PDF.

Course materials are often spread across many separate files. A university course might include lecture notes, presentation slides, reading materials, worksheets, assignments, and other documents provided throughout the semester.

When these files start to accumulate, keeping track of everything can become difficult. Combining related course materials into one PDF can make them easier to organize, store, read, and share.

Why combine course materials into one PDF?

Keeping all the materials for a course in one document can simplify your study workflow. Instead of opening multiple files every time you want to review a subject, you can have one PDF containing the documents you need.

  • Keep materials for one course in a single file.
  • Organize lecture notes and presentation slides together.
  • Prepare a single study document before an exam.
  • Share several course documents as one PDF.
  • Reduce the number of separate files in your folders.

What course materials can you combine?

A course can contain many different types of documents. If they are already available as PDF files, you can combine them into one document and arrange them in the order that makes sense for your course.

  • Lecture notes
  • Presentation slides
  • Course handouts
  • Syllabi and course guides
  • Reading materials
  • Worksheets and exercises
  • Assignments and reports
  • Study guides

Organize the files before combining them

Before creating the final PDF, decide how you want the documents to be arranged. You can organize them by lecture, week, topic, or any other structure that works for your course.

For example, a course PDF could be organized like this:

  • Course syllabus
  • Lecture 1 notes and slides
  • Lecture 2 notes and slides
  • Lecture 3 notes and slides
  • Reading materials
  • Practice exercises
  • Assignments

Putting the documents into a logical order makes the final PDF easier to use later, especially when you are reviewing the material before an exam.

Create one PDF for exam preparation

Combining course materials can be particularly useful when preparing for an exam. You can collect the relevant lecture notes, presentation slides, reading materials, and practice exercises and combine them into one study document.

You can also create separate PDF collections for different subjects or courses. For example, instead of having dozens of files for an entire semester, you can keep one organized PDF for each course.

Share course materials more easily

A single PDF can also be easier to share with classmates or colleagues. Instead of sending a large number of separate attachments, you can combine the relevant documents into one file and share it as a single attachment.

This can be useful for study groups, group projects, and other situations where several course documents need to be shared together.
